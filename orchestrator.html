The Problem

Good technology still needs to work together.

A business may rely on cloud services, its own infrastructure, security tools, applications and several specialist teams. Each can do its job well, yet the overall result can still fall short.

CoreCloud looks at the whole requirement, brings the right people together and keeps the work aligned with what the business needs.

Each component may be effective individually. Few are designed to operate as a coordinated system.

Model

From the decision to the work.

We agree on the result first. Then we work through what is needed to achieve it, from the technology and the people to the delivery plan.

Methodology

The business need comes first.

Sometimes the first question is where a workload should run. Sometimes it is about access, cost or a system that needs replacing. The starting point depends on the problem we are solving.

Once the objective is clear, we can assess the options, understand the trade-offs and bring in the right specialists. Workload placement is an important decision, but it is not always the first one.
